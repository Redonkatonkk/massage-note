# UI design and aesthetic guidelines

**English** | **[简体中文](UI_DESIGN.zh-CN.md)**

> Applies to version: `1.50.1` · Design references verified: 2026-10-02 · Guidelines reviewed: 2026-10-05

This article is the design basis for subsequent AI modifications to the front end and generation of salary settlement and individual daily statement images. Aesthetic judgment comes from store working scenes, user feedback on adjacent panels, highlight colors, member operations, phone reading and generated pictures, and existing implementations. New requirements from users are given priority; specific business rules are subject to [Product Document](../product/PRODUCT.md). For specific style values, check the source code entry below. Reason: The design rationale needs to be sustained and needs to be able to be revised with clear feedback.

<a id="审美目标"></a>

## Aesthetic goals

Massage note should be like a store work ledger that is clearly organized and can be used for a long time: quiet, clear, trustworthy, with sufficient information and straightforward operation. When the clerk is busy, he or she must quickly work record, the manager must check each transaction, and the store owner must understand the amount and payment results. The value of vision is that people can quickly find objects, understand the results, know the next step, and still feel orderly after using them for a long time. Reason: These usage tasks jointly determine the visual temperament and information density of this project.

A sense of cleanliness comes primarily from consistent alignment, stable spacing, clear text hierarchy, and well-founded grouping. Every edge, every background, and every bold should explain what it expresses. The amount of color and decoration is kept restrained, leaving the focus to records, amounts and ongoing operations. Reason: The attention span of high-frequency work pages is limited, and visual emphasis must help users make judgments.

The unified scope includes the homepage, all financial and setting subpages, personal information, login, initialization, help, offline and pop-up layers, as well as the empty list, loading, failed, deactivated and read-only status, as well as the salary settlement/individual daily statement PNG exported from the Web, and the interval settlement JPEG/individual daily statement PNG in SMS. Low-frequency pages and generated images must still follow the same set of specifications. Reason: Users will use multiple portals to complete a task, and they should be able to use the reading methods they have learned when switching pages or viewing attachments.

## How to tell if a design is appropriate

<a id="同级模块保持同一种视觉语法"></a>

### Modules at the same level maintain the same visual syntax

Adjacent modules with similar responsibilities share surfaces, borders, rounded corners, heading hierarchies, and action styles. The panel appearance of today's empty table, employee operation, gift card sales, and passenger records remains consistent, and the content is expressed through natural height and internal layout. The empty state also uses the original panel, without adding a dotted box, capsule shape or another background color due to "no data". Reason: Only when objects of the same level have the same appearance can users understand the differences as data differences.


Analytics rows normally grow to equal height with content. On desktop, the weekday/income row instead takes its height from the weekday panel; the income panel scrolls vertically and cannot stretch its peer. Preserve every period and expanded data table, provide a focusable scroll region, and let mobile panels grow naturally. Reason: long date ranges must remain accessible without adding large blank areas to the weekday comparison.

The layout can vary depending on the task. Catalogs are suitable for selecting objects, tables are suitable for comparing multiple rows, labeled records are suitable for checking one by one, and forms are suitable for editing. Reuse the existing structure first, and then determine whether you need to add a container; a group of continuous fields is usually grouped with titles, spacing, and thin lines. Reason: A common visual language needs to preserve task differences, and nesting panels repeatedly adds noise and narrows content.

<a id="信息顺序决定阅读顺序"></a>

### The order of information determines the reading order

Each page first gives the current store, date or range, and then gives the page tasks and key results. Then the objects and details are displayed, and the operations are placed immediately adjacent to the objects. There should be clear correspondence between name, amount, status, and reason, and use lighter text levels for secondary descriptions. Reason: People will first determine what they are looking at, then judge the results, and finally decide on the action.

The main actions in an operation process are highlighted once; the auxiliary actions use a lighter style, and deletion, release and transfer keep a clear distance from daily operations. Errors, amounts not entered, unsettled accounts, reasons for deletion, and historical modification prompts should appear before a decision is made, with technical or secondary information such as full JSON, number of attempts, etc. expanded on demand. Reason: The visual hierarchy should correspond to the importance of the action and the information needed to make the decision.

Member management insists on selecting an existing employee first, and then centrally viewing and modifying the employee's detailed information. The phone details are retained and returned to the roster entry, and information, salary and account status are organized into groups. Reason: Editing around a clear object at a time can reduce the search burden and mistaken modifications. The operation structure itself is also part of the neatness.

<a id="密度由内容关系决定"></a>

### Density is determined by content relationships

Labels, values, states, and operations belonging to the same object are arranged close together; leave large gaps between different tasks. Title, body text, form, and amounts are aligned to a common baseline, and field helper text stays within its own field without pulling up adjacent controls. Reason: Distance and alignment express information relationships, and users can understand groupings without reading sentence by sentence.

The homepage retains sufficient space for work records, and the desktop sidebar continues to collapse by default, expanding and covering the content when hovering or keyboard entry. Mobile phones allow content to grow naturally downwards, and fields and operations to wrap naturally. When the content becomes longer, give priority to adjusting the number of columns, grouping, and expansion levels, and avoid cramming in all the content by reducing the font size, cropping key values, or fixing the height. Reason: Space allocation must serve the actual workload, and the actual data length cannot disrupt reading.

<a id="颜色表达确定的含义"></a>

### Color expresses definite meaning

Lime white pages, white work surfaces and ink-colored text form the underlying order, and dark green is used for main operations and selection semantics. The "Not counting work schedule" button selection follows the dark green selection semantics. The work record card is still displayed according to its own payment status. It shares the right grouping with highlight and only changes its position. highlight work record uses a soft gray-green color and continues in work record cards, edits and financial details; light orange pending payment, green success, red error or dangerous operation have a clear purpose and are accompanied by text. Chart comparison sequences inherit existing semantic variables. Reason: Only when the same color continues to express the same meaning can users form stable expectations.

highlight should make records easy to find while keeping text and amounts clear and readable; highlight meaning is retained on hover, pressed, and read-only. New pages use existing color variables. When adding semantic colors, first describe the state that needs to be expressed, and then define it in the shared entry. Avoid assigning a separate set of brand colors to a module. Reason: Local emphasis cannot change the visual focus of the entire page, nor can it form a new color palette.

<a id="文字与金额承担主要表达"></a>

### Main expressions of text and amount commitment

Use system fonts, unique font sizes, and a linear icon system. The title describes the task, the label describes the field, and the description text tries to write down the executable actions as clearly as possible. Chinese, English, long names, phone numbers, serial numbers and continuous IDs are all included in the judgment of content length; key objects, amounts, deletion reasons and errors should be fully searchable. Reason: The interface mainly relies on text to complete work records and reconciliations. Real copywriting can better test the layout than the ideal length.

USD amounts use the narrow `$` symbol in both languages across pages, charts and newly generated statement images. Amounts use common formatting entries and fixed-width numbers, and similar amounts maintain consistent alignment and labeling; payroll settlements retain cents. Zero values, empty values and recorded amounts need to be clearly distinguished. Units, unentered or mixed payment prompts cannot be deleted for the sake of simplicity. The final financial definitions and allocation continue to come from the original server and domain rules. Reason: Amounts must look neat and be interpreted and verified correctly.

Generate a picture to first explain the store, date/range and employees, then highlight a main income result, then check the source by service fee and tips, and finally view the chronological details. The top of the individual daily statement displays the confirmed income; the source table only uses three columns: source, service commission, tips, two rows for cash and credit card + gift card, and the next two columns arrange the same transactions as the daily work record card. Each card separately explains the service fee before discount, actual income split, tips and employee income, cash without frame, card with frame, gift card with text, retain highlights, discounts and add-on. The balance to be checked out indicates that it has not been included in the summary, the unknown shows "unconfirmed", and the value of zero amount is retained; the income data cannot imply that wages have been paid. Reason: Images are often read in mobile messages, and clear order and field names help people understand amounts better than colors or borders.

## How to judge the layout of phones

On the work-record page at 760px and below, an overflowing open-day employee track returns to show half of the preceding work card on the left and the entire Add record card. This keeps a pending-payment preview next to the next entry action; the preceding card retains its actual payment status. If space is insufficient, prioritize the complete Add record card; do not add overflow when all cards fit. Preserve the ten-second idle return, pointer-drag protection, reduced motion and existing card order. Desktop keeps its right-group preview. Implementation: [record-track scrolling](../../apps/web/lib/record-track-scroll.ts). Reason: phones need both the current record context and a fully usable next-entry action.

The order of tasks on the phone remains the same as that on the desktop, and the specific arrangement is adjusted according to the available width. The ledger, recycle bin, sending queue and expenditure details that are checked item by item use vertical records with field names: the object and date come first, the key amount and status follow, the reason and error can be read directly, the secondary details are expanded as needed, and finally this operation. Reason: Narrow screens need to keep labels and values adjacent to each other to reduce the need for swiping back and forth to find corresponding fields.

Weekly schedules, cross-day comparisons, ordinary column charts and heat maps can retain the matrix and independent horizontal scrolling. The operating analysis curve, weekly average revenue and turnover revenue charts are adapted to the fixed container width, and provide necessary fixed row and column labels or instructions. All scrolling is limited to the corresponding container, and the page itself remains at normal width. The long editing pop-up layer keeps the closed entrance accessible, and the content scrolls independently; the input font size and touch area follow the common baseline. Reason: These structures need to express row-column relationships, clear context and sufficient operating areas to support phone reading.

You cannot use "media query exists" or "color variables are consistent" to infer that the phone is readable. Verify the arrangement of objects, field names, values, states, prompts, and actions in the DOM page by page, covering long copy, multiple sources of amounts, empty lists, and failure states. Reason: Responsive style and reading structure are two aspects that need to be examined separately.

## Subsequent AI work sequence

1. Read this page, related product rules and existing components, and first write down who the page serves, what the current object is, and what the next step is. Reason: When the task is ambiguous, it is easy to use decoration instead of layout judgment.
2. Select the existing directory, table, vertical record or form structure according to the task, and then determine the content sequence and primary and secondary operations. Reason: Layout should be based on information first, and style should express hierarchy later.
3. Reuse shared components, colors, rounded corners, controls and icons, check actual CSS loading order and subsequent breakpoint overrides. Reason: Local similarities may still produce different appearances in other states.
4. Checks desktop, mobile, low-frequency subpages and popups, as well as long names/English, key amounts, loading, empty status and errors. Reason: The complete content and status determine whether the design can continue to be used.
5. Follow the [Development Guide](DEVELOPMENT.md) to do verification commensurate with the changes, and explain the verification scope during the handover. By default, only code and command line/API checks are performed; the browser is used only when explicitly requested by the user. Reason: Continuing the user's verification preferences while avoiding writing automated checks as visual or physical acceptance.

Before completion, you should be able to explain: what each emphasis element expresses, why elements at the same level are consistent, how users map amounts to objects, whether the next step on phones is clear, and whether low-frequency pages follow common standards. If the explanation is unclear, modify the structure or reduce unfounded emphasis. Reason: These questions turn aesthetic requirements into reviewable design choices.

## Direction and Source

The interface is organized around the store's high-frequency work record, reconciliation and personnel maintenance. Lime white background, white working surface, ink text and dark green main operation form the basic level; the edges and white space are responsible for grouping, and the elastic layer only uses shadows. Get rid of decorative gradients, card floats, and large areas of brand color. Gray-green highlight, light orange pending payment, green success and red error still have clear meanings, and status is not only determined by color. Reason: The surface level is used for high-frequency work. The status colors have their own meanings and require text assistance.

The source of the existing design is as follows; only the layout principles are borrowed, and the code rewrite of the member toolbar has a license statement.

- [Radix font size system](https://github.com/radix-ui/themes/blob/1faff10ac26ae17f09944d418c6949b93fc6b566/packages/radix-ui-themes/src/styles/tokens/typography.css) and [Spacing system](https://github.com/radix-ui/themes/blob/1faff10ac26ae17f09944d418c6949b93fc6b566/packages/radix-ui-themes/src/styles/tokens/space.css): Unify the font size level and organize the spacing according to 4/8/12/16/24/32px.
- [Cal Settings Header](https://github.com/calcom/cal.diy/blob/54343aa685ae8f33159d2f485ec4a57bad5c574a/packages/features/settings/appDir/SettingsHeader.tsx): The title, purpose description and main operations form a stable header, and use thin edges for demarcation.
- [Twenty Member Settings](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty/blob/2fedea0fa394c9f4997ca46d157956f4e562a748/packages/twenty-front/src/pages/settings/members/SettingsWorkspaceMembers.tsx): Members, invitations and permissions have different task boundaries to avoid stacking all operations at the same time.
- The adaptation of shadcn/ui in the original member search toolbar continues to be retained, and its license can be found in [Third Party Notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). Reason: MIT requires that copyright and license be retained on distributed rewritten code.

The basic font size is 16px, the main text is usually 14–16px, the auxiliary text is 12–14px, the page title is about 28px, and the amount uses monospaced numbers. The rounded corners of buttons and input boxes are 6px, and the main work panel is 10px; the touch height of high-frequency main operations is at least 44px. Fonts use local system fonts and do not rely on third-party font requests. Reason: This is the current style variable and touch baseline. The old nineteen-pixel and large rounded corner rules have no corresponding implementation.

The identity field for selling cards and gift card payments shares the same `gift-card-identity-field.tsx`: the label is on top, the input box is on the same line as the mode button, and the narrow part is naturally wrapped. The selected mode uses dark green and sets `aria-pressed`; the user name is entered in text, and the amount and operations maintain the original order. Reason: Numeric user names and card numbers need to be clearly distinguished, and mode switching cannot squeeze the phone input or change the corresponding relationship of payment amounts.

## Style maintenance rules

- Color variables and basic rules are in `globals.css`, shared visuals/animations are in `design-system.css`, and media breakpoints are in `responsive.css`, loaded in this order. Reason: In actual cascading dependency order, writing independent surfaces again across files will overwrite the shared design.
- `.board-panel` is shared by work record empty table, employee operation, card selling and runner; breakpoints only adjust the layout, mobile card selling/runner are single columns. Reason: These modules use the same work surface and the old "always side by side" does not comply with the current breakpoint.
- The native text, date, month and drop-down box use a common baseline, the fieldset title/content are laid out separately, and the top of the field is aligned. Reason: Native semantics and disabled propagation must be preserved, and auxiliary instructions must not stretch adjacent controls.
- Use shared variables for color and rounded corners, and run `pnpm ui:check` and `pnpm ui:mobile` to modify the style. Reason: The former checks the variable source, and the latter checks the surface, field, touch height and layout of the phone breakpoint according to the actual loading sequence, and covers the four data combinations of selling cards/walkouts; the degradation sample verification checker can find coverage issues.
- The salary settlement and individual daily statement images are each shared by a shared SVG unified web export and SMS attachment, sharing the theme, text wrapping and surface drawing: lime white background, white surface, ink text, dark green emphasis, thin edges and uniform rounded corners, without using decorative gradients, shadows, red-brown background colors or excessive font weight. Organize by "object and date → main income → source summary → transaction-by-transaction details", the main income will only be highlighted once; service fees, tips, employee income and guest payments will be marked separately, and the amounts will be retained in cents. The store name, name, item, payment split and long amount are completely wrapped, and the picture increases with the content. Reason: The attachment is also the same work ledger. The color, reading level and field correspondence need to continue the page semantics at the same time. The complete amount and object can be used for verification.

## Navigation and header

Starting from 1200px, the 64px icon sidebar is used by default. When the mouse is moved or the keyboard focus is entered, it expands to 216px, displaying the name and language switching. The subdirectories of finance and store settings are collapsed by default. The parent item uses native buttons. Click once to expand and click again to collapse. The two groups are independently controlled. The expanded state is expressed by `aria-expanded`. Click on the child item to enter the corresponding page. Automatically retracts when the mouse is moved away and there is no keyboard focus; normal focus left by a mouse click will not prevent retraction. Expand the overlay content without changing the width of the main area or causing horizontal jumps; only 64px is reserved for the main area, and the 1600px maximum width limit of the desktop is cancelled. Collapse entries retain accessible names, and hidden submenus and language buttons do not enter the tab order. Small screens continue to use the same set of entrance bottom navigation and horizontal page labels. Role permissions, store parameters and URL partition recovery are determined by the original navigation helper function. Reason: Collapse and expansion share the same navigation state, and overlay expansion prevents the main area of the work record from jumping.

The finance and settings page headers directly display the title and task description of the current subpage; the desktop submenu and the small screen label share the selected state. The language switch is at the bottom of the desktop sidebar, and the small screen is in the global tool area; the login and initialization pages continue to retain the language entry. The AI assistant continues to use the work record, financial navigation portal and body portal. The pop-up layer is located on the right side of the sidebar, and the soft keyboard and focus fallback logic are retained. Reason: The same route and role menu avoid maintaining a set of behaviors for desktop, small screen and language portal.

Monthly Summary places a wrapping row of Non-highlighted / Highlighted native checkboxes and a Search button immediately below the month/export toolbar. Both options default to selected; edits apply only on Search, and an empty selection disables Search with an explanation. Keep export and report sections on the applied condition. A wide daily trailing 30-day revenue comparison sits beside payment shares; the three-month metric table takes its own full-width row. Remove the employee earnings comparison panel and keep employee subtotals last. At 1100px use four metrics and paired trend/payment panels; below 600px use single-column metrics and labeled comparison records showing all three date ranges. Keep shared green semantic colors, system fonts, surfaces and radii. Reason: wider month comparisons improve reading and preserve the distinction between rolling averages, monthly results and individual reconciliation.

Mark the maximum with its amount, date, and series; recompute when curve choices change. Hover, focus, and click readouts also show the actual daily revenue, including zero and an explicit not-closed state; actual revenue does not enter the average scale. Other charts retain their existing axes. Reason: small changes in averages should remain readable without treating missing samples as zero.

## Responsive record display
