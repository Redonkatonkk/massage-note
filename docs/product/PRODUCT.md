## Product Scope and Data Principles

- Provides multiple stores, three roles, Chinese and English responsive Web work records and business accounting, and supports login on phones, iPads, computers and multiple devices. Reason: This is the scope of the product supported by existing pages, member scopes, and live interfaces.
- The lasting amount uses integer cents, the commission uses cents, and the final financial calculation uses the domain function; the display format does not change the ledger. Reason: Floating point numbers, front-end totals, and model answers cannot be used as amount truth.
- Ordinary work records and overviews usually display whole dollars; salary settlements, expenses, reconciliation pictures, etc. use the existing precise format, and the editor retains the real cents and does not automatically add `.00`. Reason: `formatUsd` and `formatUsdPrecise` have different purposes, and generally requiring all read-only amounts to be rounded will lose reconciliation precision.
- The work record saves the name, duration, price, discount, commission and source, salary, time zone and business day snapshot; directory changes do not overwrite the closed historical days. Reason: Historical accounts need to be traced back to the content at the time of occurrence, and the current day's recalculation is allowed and controlled services will be provided.
- Business objects such as work records, sales cards, wages, directories, and members continue to use soft deletion and recovery; the key is to write and save the operator, before and after values, request number, and transaction audit/outbox. Reason: Recovery and accounting traceability rely on these records; physical removal of inactive employee rows is the clear established exception.
- The language defaults to Simplified Chinese, and you can choose to save it locally; the English display can translate the custom directory name, and the editing and historical snapshots still retain the original text. Reason: Language switching is a display and cannot be an account modification operation.

## Accounts, stores and members

- Use a US ten-digit number, internally standardized to E.164; Firebase will verify the phone number and set a name and password for the first time. Old users can log in with a password or verification code. Reason: The current authentication link supports both Phone Auth and password exchange for Custom Token, and the process of only recording the verification code cannot continue.
- Supports logging out of the device and revoking all sessions; the common logout entrance is concentrated on the "My" page, retaining the local Firebase trusted status and preferences, and the same number can be restored to log in. Reason: Exiting is a low-frequency account operation and does not occupy today's work record tool area; the two exits have different effects. To unbind a shared device, you must cancel all sessions or clear browser data.
- The login resend countdown, reCAPTCHA and interface rate limiting are still implemented according to the current implementation; the account status interface returns whether it exists and whether a password is set. Reason: The login page selects the registration or password process accordingly. The "never disclose whether to register or not" and the independent error locking promise of the old document do not comply with the current interface.
- Create a store using a globally unique six-digit code, name, time zone and global default commission, and then set the directory; the default time zone comes from the device. Reason: The store code is responsible for adding positioning, and the store time zone is responsible for date calculation without device context.
- The same account can hold different roles in different stores, and each store has a unique Owner; the Manager can manage members, directories, work records, and finances, and can also designate managers, but cannot modify/remove the Owner, transfer the store owner, or delete the store. Reason: Permissions are granted by membership, and ownership has a separate atomic transfer process.
- The transfer target of the store owner must be a member of the store who is currently employed and associated with a valid account; the role is independent of whether to participate in the work record. Reason: Unclaimed/deactivated accounts cannot be taken over, and management permissions should not determine whether services can be provided.
- Managers who participate in the work record enter their wages and arrears normally; the Owner's service income is accounted separately and does not enter the salary settlement for himself or the boss's outstanding arrears. Reason: Operating income and employee debt are different measures.
- All active members can view and modify the work record of the day, including other employees; the employee history can only be read by the individual, and cannot read the entire store's finances, manage the directory, or perform settlement. Reason: The privacy of today's collaborative work record and historical salary is protected by different permission boundaries.
- The store owner/manager can pre-create unregistered members, the name is required, the account number can be empty, work record and configuration commission are allowed; full-time/part-time is required when daily ranking is opened. Reason: Existing member IDs carry history, and the ranking algorithm requires additional labor types.
- When joining, register according to the account number. The First Name must accurately match the standardized name of the member to be claimed, and the original member must be successfully bound. Otherwise, submit for approval; temporary names cannot be used to falsely claim. Reason: Keep the original ID, work record and finance, while preventing the form name from becoming an identity credential.
- Active display names are unique after normalization; deactivating only deactivates membership and restores the preservation of history. Reason: The same name will make the directory and robot positioning ambiguous, and deleting the account or history will damage the audit.
- For member management, select employees first and then edit; partition information/salary, project commission, account status, add, approve, deactivate, and restore using independent confirmation forms; save and call only the changed interface. Reason: The current task boundaries and version interfaces of `members-panel.tsx` need to be maintained separately.
- Protect drafts by cutting employees, partitions or leaving the page; real-time new versions/deactivations do not overwrite input, reload and check after conflicts, and retain the submitted version when the data is successful but the commission fails. Reason: The two save interfaces cannot be repeatedly overwritten with old versions, nor can input be lost due to synchronization.
- Enter the U.S. ten-digit SMS number and save it as E.164; the dedicated number is given priority, and the registered number will fall back when empty. You must have a valid number to enable reception. Reason: Unclaimed members may not have registration numbers, and sending tasks cannot rely on sample placeholder values.

## Business day, calendar and writing

- The natural date in the device time zone is taken today, and the date is changed at midnight; the new work record is assigned to the date according to the start time, and the date does not change when it ends across midnight; no device request returns to the store time zone. Reason: `device-time.ts` and `business-day.ts` no longer use the closing deadline backward date.
- Existing records will only be restored when the modification start time is clear; the old cut-off field remains compatible with interfaces and snapshots. Reason: Changing the amount cannot move the historical business day, and the old client and database still read this field.
- Store owners/managers can view future work schedules and not closed historical days; employees can only view today and their past dates, but cannot change history; work records, runners, daily closing and cash settlements still have their own restrictions on future writing. Reason: The permission to schedule work in advance is not equal to the permission to book in advance.
- The closed work record first cancels the daily closing and then modifies it; the cancellation or related modification rolls back the cash settlement, the payroll ledger retains and recalculates the outstanding debt, and the operation is written for audit. Reason: The wages paid are real payments and cannot be automatically deleted due to re-opening of accounts.
- Write retention permissions, object ownership, business day locks, versions, idempotency and transactions; after 409, it will be checked by the user, and the old fields will not be automatically raised and the version will be reissued. Reason: Concurrency, replay, and cross-store requests cannot cause half-billing or overwrite new data.
- The calendar marks the dates when the work record is valid but not closed; the closed date displays the discounted service performance minus the gift card service fee and then sells the card to receive the actual payment. Employees only have to have the work record date and amount, and cancel the daily closing to restore the dot. Reason: Date stamps also have financial information and must be segregated by role.
- The work record page is cached by store/month for up to twelve months, and requests from the same month are merged; directories and members are reused across dates, and only the target table is re-read; local writing, manual refresh, cross-day invalidation, automatic synchronization/reconnection only refreshes business, and is read when the financial calendar is opened. Reason: The current cache explicitly updates calendar changes for other devices with manual refresh, and old automatic expiration requests will re-generate duplicate requests.

## Catalog, commissions and discounts

- The main projects include names, abbreviations and multiple non-overlapping duration price brackets, sharing sorting, activation status and project commissions; add-ons can have independent amounts, durations and commissions. Reason: The price belongs to the duration range, and the project identity and exclusive commission should not be split into multiple objects at any time.
- The add-on allows customization of this order, leaving it blank/zero minutes without adding time, and 1-720 minutes to save snapshots; the API clearly indicates `null` means not adding time, and the directory will default if it is omitted. Reason: This order adjustment has different semantics from the default value.
- The standard commissions are sequentially exclusive for employee projects, employee default, project default, and store default; custom projects only take employee default and then store default. Reason: Domain functions parse and save the final source in this order.
- Employees cannot change the commission of this order, but the manager can modify and audit it; save the employee commission and recalculate it to the current day if it is not closed, and the relevant cash settlement will be rolled back, and closed and earlier dates will be retained. Reason: The current day adjustment is a controlled exception and cannot be generalized to rewrite history.
- Fixed and proportional discounts can be used in multiple items. A positive number indicates a reduction or exemption without approval; the ratio is 0-100%, with a maximum of two decimal places. Each item is calculated based on the same pre-discount item plus add-on amount and rounded to cents. The total does not exceed the service fee base. Reason: The ratio does not include tips, nor is it calculated based on compound interest on the balance after successive deductions.
- The discount is borne by the store and does not reduce wages; both the proportion and amount are saved as snapshots, and the proportion is recalculated when the current single item changes. Catalog changes do not change the history in batches. Reason: The salary base is independent of post-discount performance.
- Automatic discounts are generated from Monday to Thursday when the store is open, non-highlighted and the pre-discount amount reaches the threshold. It can be used in conjunction with manual discounts; single removal/restoration of persistent storage disabled status. Reason: Based on the judgment that the web page and the robot are shared, a single deactivation should not close the entire store rule.
- highlight Create/edit to exclude automatic discounts, cancel highlight to re-judge but not lift manual deactivation; manual discounts and actual payments are retained. Reason: highlight affects discounts and handling fees, and display switching cannot be regarded as re-collection.

## Work record and payment

- Quick entry offers the unique durations from all enabled, non-deleted services, sorted from shortest to longest. Set the start time and optional highlight or exclusion from rotation, then click a duration to save immediately without service selection or confirmation. It creates no service, price or commission snapshot. These records count as services and occupy the selected time while remaining pending; their price and wages are unfilled until a service is chosen. With zero or blank receipts, details may save without a service; positive receipts require a preset or custom service before payment confirmation. When first choosing a service, retain the quick-entry duration if supported; otherwise use 60 minutes or the first catalog option. Reason: the work time is already known, while the service and financial facts must remain undecided until entered.
- Detailed entry sits in the quick-entry header above Close, which stays in its original action row. It opens the full editor with the current employee, time, highlight and rotation flag, prefilled with 60 minutes and no service, without saving a record; closing discards this new form. Save creates the service, details and optional payment atomically. Reason: entering the detailed flow must not leave unwanted records or partial writes.
- Quick entry uses one row for exactly three duration options on desktop and phones, and two columns for other counts. In record details, switching services selects 60 minutes when available or the first catalog option otherwise, updating its price and end time. Existing records retain their historical duration and valid drafts. Reason: all quick-entry durations must remain visible, while detail defaults must preserve saved and manually selected facts.
- "Not counting work" is located on the left side of the highlight in the details, and is located between the placeholder and the highlight in the fast work record. After saving, it will be arranged from right to left together with the highlight work record by time, and it will return to the left after canceling. This mark does not set highlight, and the small card will still display the normal style according to its own payment status, and the number of work records and the entire amount rules will not be affected. Saving a placeholder omits this flag. Reason: The financial semantics of schedule display grouping and highlight are different and need to be saved independently and support cross-device recovery.
- Clicking Placeholder in quick entry immediately saves a placeholder at the selected time. The reserved time is used for date attribution and card sorting. No items are selected or highlighted. After saving, the small card displays two X diagonal lines connecting the four corners and covering the entire card. The selected time is displayed at the top. Clicking only provides deletion confirmation. Placeholders are not counted in work record number, work occupancy, finance, salary, daily closing and operating analysis, nor do they generate financial calendar marks. Reason: The placeholder only expresses that this job skips the employee and cannot be disguised as zero-dollar service or pending checkout.
- The placeholder is saved as `PLACEHOLDER` and does not create projects, add-ons, discounts or payment details; it only supports soft deletion and manager recovery, and cannot be edited, paid or converted to a normal work record. Create, delete and restore without rollback and still respect permissions, business day locks, versions, idempotency, auditing and synchronization. Reason: Placeholder order needs to be preserved across devices, and financial status should not change due to no-income tags.
- The same employee is allowed to have multiple pending accounts; the records are sorted by the start time, and the work status is the latest end time of the current record in progress. It will be idle when it is expected to end. Reason: The payment status is different from whether the service is being served, and the robot occupation pointer cannot represent the work record of all web pages.
- The start and end times of the web details share the same explicit service date, and changes in items or overtime are linked to the current total duration; the manual end is calculated back to the start, and corrections are prompted for cross-day/end earlier than the start. Even if the API omits the end, the compatibility logic of retaining the actual duration plus the configuration difference will still be used, and it is clear that `endAt` takes precedence. Reason: The current front-end total duration linkage is completely different from the old client API. The document cannot promise unified recalculation that does not exist in the back-end.
- Clicking Save or pressing Enter in details uses the same validation and save flow; notes retain line breaks, and composition/control activation does not save. Zero or blank cash/card service fees and tips save as pending, even for a previously confirmed record; positive gift card service fees or tips count as receipts. Returning to pending removes the payment snapshot and clears derived receipt, tip and income fields while retaining service prices and commissions. Other receipts validate and confirm together with the details. Unassigned pending records support metadata edits without creating a service snapshot; an explicitly unknown duration remains unknown until supplied. Reason: no receipts must not imply checkout, and details/payment transitions must commit atomically.
- Blank and explicit zero remain distinct draft values, but both count as zero for the detail-save status decision. When confirming nonzero receipts, specify at least one service-fee field; remaining methods and empty tips become zero. The explicit confirm-payment API keeps its existing zero-payment compatibility. Reason: the detail form infers pending from receipts while existing explicit API confirmations retain their contract.
- When using gift card, you must fill in the serial number or switch "Use user name" to fill in the user name. At least one of gift card service fee or tips is greater than zero; when not in use, the serial number is empty and the amount is zero; cash/swipe card/gift card are allowed to be mixed. Reason: Ledger association with cash payroll allocation requires full payment split.
- Projects, add-ons, discounts and various payments must not be negative. The actual service fee may be inconsistent with the discounted performance. Only warnings and confirmations are allowed. Reason: Currently, fact records such as undercharge, overcharge, and order exemption are supported. There is no independent business process for negative refunds.
- The main number of work record card service fee shows the items before discount plus add-on, and the payment area shows confirmed receipt or pending confirmation of receivables; tips are separated independently, with card swiping and frame, cash without frame, and gift card logo. Discount is marked with `off`, and add-on and remarks are marked separately. Reason: The original price, receivables, actual receipts and wages cannot be mixed into one number.
- The confirmed service payment balance is displayed abnormally, and is mutually exclusive with the pending checkout mark; highlight continues to use gray-green, and hover/read-only maintains the color. Reason: Status should be identified by both text and markup, and the style should not obscure financial exceptions.
- Deletion only confirms and does not force input of reasons; soft deletion excludes ordinary pages and statistics, and the administrator restores and retains deleted snapshots. Reason: Recovery still requires daily closing locks and versions, and deletion cannot damage the audit content.

## Employee rows, weekly schedules and rotations

- Managers can add employees, and the empty table allows multiple people to add in the order of checking; if it fails midway, the follow-up will be stopped, the successful items will be refreshed, and the unfinished selection will be retained; employees can only add themselves when they go to work. Reason: This UI batch inherits write-by-write and cannot be mistakenly promised to be the same as the robot atomic batch.
- Store owners/managers can hide or restore historical employee rows, and closed historical days is also allowed; historical blank rows and shifts are retained for restoration, and work record, financial, and daily closing snapshots remain unchanged. If there is no work record or placeholder (including deletion history) today/in the future, the rows and shifts will still be hidden and removed. If there is a record, it will only be hidden. Today/in the future, closed must be canceled first. Reason: Historical attendance correction cannot delete business history or change closed accounts, and the current blank row added by mistake can still be cleared.
- The employee order is moved up/down, and the records move with the entire row; the weekly template only manages active service personnel, repeating from Monday to Sunday, and it will take effect immediately after saving. Reason: The current interface does not provide drag sorting, and template initialization and manual ordering have independent sources.
- The template is only initialized on the current day, and updated according to the latest list on future days, and history/closed is skipped; canceling the arrangement only removes automatic rows without work record/shift, and manually adds and retains them. Reason: Template updates cannot delete actual attendance or labor schedules.
- Manual removal on a single day takes precedence over the template, and refresh will not add it back; the relative order will be retained after manual reordering in the future, new members will be added, and automatic updates will be resumed only after active regeneration; no version/audit will be written if there are no changes. Reason: The Kanban audit version records the latest human intent, and refreshing cannot overwrite it.
- The pop-up window "Apply" covers all schedules for the selected today/future date and does not save the template; any work records (including deletion history), closed or past dates will be rolled back. Reason: A one-time overwrite should not break the work record association, nor should the result be replaced by an automatic template.
- Daily opening ranking is turned off by default; turning it on requires all service personnel to be full-time/part-time, and managers can generate today/future sequences without changing the work record one by one. Reason: Rotation only organizes employees and does not assign workers one by one.
- Hidden in the schedule will be regarded as not working on that day, and will be re-incorporated after recovery; each person's most recent visible attendance position will be taken in rotation (the hidden date will be skipped, and the position will be calculated based on visible employees on that day), the original second and subsequent ones will be moved forward by one, the original first will be ranked after other people with history, and those without history will be last; in the same position, full-time priority will be given, the latest date will be given priority, and the stable member ID will be given priority. History hiding/restoration affects subsequent generation and future automatic scheduling. Today's first automatic generation uses the latest history; today's existing sequences need to be actively regenerated, and future manual sequencing protection will continue to take effect. Reason: `rankRotationCandidates` requires deterministic results, and attendance corrections cannot silently override manual orders.
- Explanation snapshots are saved when generated; reasons are not rewritten for manual ordering or data changes, and old empty snapshots are not backfilled by speculation; administrators can export complete PNGs in the current language, and super-long sub-images. Reason: The explanation should reproduce decisions made at the time, rather than recalculate history using today's data.

## Gift card and Walkout

- Physical card sales are independent of employee work records. Each store defaults to an atomic increment from 1001. You can also submit a custom number of up to 120 characters. After Unicode normalization, case and blank spaces are ignored, and historical numbers are deleted and not reused. Reason: Concurrency and historical duplication prevention cannot be guaranteed by the front-end suggestion number.
- Online card selling is switched through the button on the right side of the serial number. The user name is saved as text. Numbers, leading zeros and repeated purchases are allowed. The system account number is not queried and the physical card serial number is not assigned. Payments can be recorded directly by the user name, without the need for existing sales. The usernames are accurately grouped after removing the leading and trailing spaces, and are separated from the physical cards of the same number. Repeated purchases will not accumulate the accumulated usage amount. Reason: The external card selling identity is just an account mark and cannot be used to impersonate the system account number or physical card number.
- The gift card ledger uses one desktop table with separate physical-card and online-sale sections. Online purchases share the sale columns, with username and usage records spanning repeated purchases; accounts without registered sales remain visible. Mobile records retain the same sections. Reason: sales and usage can be checked together while keeping physical serial numbers separate from usernames and showing each account's usage once.
- When selling a card, save the face value, discount threshold/proportion and amount snapshot, actual cash received/card swipe and operator; the total actual receipt must be greater than zero and can be different from the payable after discount, and a reminder will be displayed when saving. Reason: Payable is a pricing rule, and income must be based on the actual money received.
- All actual proceeds from card sales go into the store, and no employee wages or cash settlements are allocated; use the card service fee plus tips to write off the expenditure for the gift card. Reason: The actual proceeds from card sales are included in revenue at the time of sale, and the amount of card services is not included repeatedly; the write-off details are retained and split into services and tips.
- Gift cards can be used across multiple transactions, and the balance will not be automatically written off; confirmed valid uses are grouped according to standardized numbers, and old cards without sales registration are also entered into the ledger, but the sales information is unknown and no additional income will be generated. Reason: The existing ledger records usage facts and does not have a complete balance account.
- Selling cards inherits the current day/history permissions, daily closing lock, version, idempotency, soft deletion and audit of the work record. Reason: Sales also form accounts and cannot bypass business day protection.
- Walkout records the time on the device wall, 1-999 people (one person by default) and up to 500 characters to remove blank comments; editable or soft deletion, not writable in the future, closed and read-only; currently all members can read and write, history is only for administrators. Reason: Number of people, time and notes are independent business facts that require versioning and auditing but do not form financial amounts.
- The `Walk-in` button of the Walkout form can switch directly to the store source. After saving, a walk icon will be displayed in the upper right corner of the card, and editing can be canceled; old records and new records are not marked by default, and the source will still be displayed after daily closing. Reason: The store arrival method is a business fact that needs to be saved persistently, independent of notes, headcount and financial statistics.
- Walkout only enters business quantity statistics and does not add work records, income, wages or cards sold. Reason: There is no service payment for unsettled customers.

## Amount and operating summary

When the actual service fee paid by the gift card is higher than the discounted performance, revenue and the average value will be kept as negative numbers and rounded to cents without being truncated to zero; unfilled gift card payments will be treated as zero. Reason: The project allows actual payments and payables to be different, and historical facts cannot be hidden by aggregation.

Let P be the main item, E be the add-on total, and D be the discount; CS/CC/GCS are cash/card/gift card service fee, and CT/CCT/GCT are the corresponding tips. The following formulas are performed by domain functions.

| Caliber | Formula/Constraint | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Service fee base | P + E | Salary and original card price are calculated based on the amount before discount. |
| Service fee performance after discount | P + E − D | Discount affects performance and cannot rewrite actual receipts. |
| Actual service fee | CS + CC + GCS | The payment ratio requires three methods to form the denominator. |
| tips/guest payment | CT + CCT + GCT; actual service fee + tips | tips are separate from service fee, and wages and cash are attributed differently. |
| service commission | Amount of each project × respective final commission, totaled after rounding off | Multiple projects can have different commissions, and cannot be multiplied by one proportion as a whole. |
| Total employee income | service commission + all tips | Discounts are borne by the store. |
| revenue | service fee performance after discount − GCS + actual payment received from selling cards | Actual revenue will be included when selling cards, card services will not be counted repeatedly, and tips will be calculated separately; service performance and wages will not be affected. |
| gift card write-off expenditure | GCS + GCT | gift card services and tips consume the card funds already received by the store. |
| Today's turnover | revenue − GCT, that is, service fee performance after discount + actual receipts from selling cards − All gift card write-offs | The existing amount of the daily subtotal will be used without increasing tips income. Gift card services and tips will only be deducted once. |
| Store revenue | revenue + cash tips + card tips − employee income, that is, today’s turnover + all tips − employee income | revenue has already deducted the gift card service fee, and only excluded gift card tips, and will no longer deduct the entire write-off. |
| Kanban total revenue | Store revenue + Owner service revenue + Manager service revenue | Manager service revenue is included in operating accounting, expenditures are counted independently, and negative numbers are allowed. |
| Total daily subtotal income | Store income + Owner service income + Manager service income | The income of managers as service personnel is included in the operating accounting, and no handling fees are deducted. |
| Credit card processing fee | The total amount of the selected ordinary card swipe is × 2.5%, rounded to cents; each highlight including the selected card swipe amount is $3 | Partial swipes are also counted; there is no charge for no swipe card, highlight zero swipe card, gift card and selling card swipe. |
| Total financial settlement income | Store income + Owner service income + Manager service income − Credit card handling fee | Different from the definition before the billboard handling fee, gift card net income cannot be subtracted or subtracted once. |

- Today's total revenue on the board is only summarized based on work record and gift card revenue and expenditure, and the monthly total/average daily expenditure on the expenditure page is not read or deducted; adding, modifying, deleting and restoring expenditures does not change the revenue on the board. Reason: The expenditure ledger is managed independently, and revenue and costs are no longer linked.
- The store summary is only given to the Owner/Manager, and is calculated based on the undeleted work record. Hidden rows are not affected; the dashboard contains known wages/tips to be settled, and the individual's confirmed income is paid separately. Reason: Interface visibility and confirmation level are not in the same statistical range.
- Monthly Summary shows monthly turnover, monthly store income, monthly total income after card fees, and service record count. The full-width comparison table covers the selected month and preceding two months; employee earnings comparison bars are removed, while employee subtotals and detail/SMS controls remain last. Employee access stays self-scoped. Amounts reuse server totals and retain cents. Reason: monthly review needs comparable store results first and individual reconciliation below.
- Daily trailing 30-day average revenue compares the selected and previous months by day number using the analytics API’s average30Cents and sample count. Each closed date includes itself and the preceding 29 calendar days, including dates before month start. Open dates stay gaps; empty closed dates count as zero. Do not average daily turnover or truncate samples at month start. Only Owner/Manager request store-wide analytics. Reason: the existing revenue definition and window must remain consistent across screens and access roles.
- The current month ends at the store business date and compares the preceding two months through the same day number, capped at each month end. Historical selections compare three full calendar months. Label every table column’s dates and retain change versus the immediately preceding month. Queries, details and CSV use all payment/amount/highlight sources; employee details narrow only the employee. Each comparison or average request can fail and retry independently, without replacing missing data with zero or accepting a stale month response. Reason: comparable dates, financial definitions and isolated failures prevent misleading growth and stale figures.
- The sales card only includes managers, unlimited employees, and store inquiries with the entire amount and does not limit only highlight; it will not be allocated to employees. The number of items is the work record plus the number of cards sold, and the total payment from the customer includes the actual receipts from card sales. Reason: Sales belong to the store, employee or single amount filter cannot be mixed with unassignable receipts.
- The employee subtotal shows the difference between the current default commission and the exclusive ratio, and is not deduced from the salary; the daily subtotal is located in the business analysis, following the independent date and highlight conditions of the analysis page, retaining the date, week, turnover, average reference and composition details of the entire store in the past thirty days, and the front end is not calculated separately. Reason: Historical wages may use multiple snapshot ratios, and the date and conditions on the analysis page must correspond to the daily verification results.
- The last 30-day average of the Kanban and daily rows is only displayed when the day is closed, and the window is the day and the previous 29 days; only the closed day is counted as the denominator, and the empty daily closing is counted as zero. It is not supplemented outside the window and is not subject to financial filtering/start date truncation. Reason: This is a store-wide operating reference, which is different from the currently filtered summary average.
- The comparison table’s daily average uses closed dates within each displayed period, independently of the chart’s rolling 30-day window; no samples show —. Revenue includes card sale receipts, deducts gift card service fees, excludes tips, and rounds to cents. Reason: a period average and rolling average answer different questions, and open days are not zero samples.
- The current export is to prevent formula injection into UTF-8 CSV, individual daily statement PNG, and interval settlement pictures; Excel/PDF/printable cash slips are not currently implemented. Reason: The documentation cannot treat the old product direction as a supported feature.

## Cash, daily full settlement and payroll ledger

- Cash corresponds to service commission according to this order service commission × cash service fee / all paid service fees are apportioned; if the actual receipt is zero, the cash salary is zero, and the remaining salary is still to be paid. Reason: Mixed payment and free items cannot use fixed 60% or divide by zero.
- The actual salary received in cash is min (cash service fee, cash corresponding salary), which should be left as the actual salary plus cash tips, and should be paid as cash service fee minus the actual salary received, and the gap continues to be unpaid. Reason: Money not received by the employee cannot be recorded as earned, nor can a cash submission be negative.
- Cash settlement only includes those with valid work records on the day, and supports single/full settlement, rollback, historical details and audit; the status is only open/settled, and partial payment or net balance of both parties is not recorded. ## Real-time, AI and product boundaries

- SSE notification reread REST, normally about two to three seconds plus request time; retain date, scroll and draft, hide/offline pause, resume supplementary reading; no message reconnect for ten seconds, low frequency retry if failed. Reason: Events are not ledgers, and a successful connection does not mean that the data has been synchronized.
- "Synchronized" requires actual messages/heartbeats and recent REST success, continuous change merging, and read changes queuing; old requests will become invalid during store cut/uninstall. Reason: A failed read or expired response cannot overwrite the new context.
- Web AI first understands and saves the complete server preview, user confirmation, re-authentication and one-time consumption; details, payment, audit and preview consumption are the same transactions, and the secondary confirmation is deleted. Reason: Models cannot directly grant permissions or execute arbitrary SQL.
- Sessions are isolated by store, user, and assistant, and history helps understand inquiries and finance to recheck the latest data; permission changes do not inherit the old scope, and front-end refreshes do not automatically restore sessions. Reason: Neither historical replies nor unexecuted previews are current writing or financial facts.
- AI Finance only calls deterministic statistics and explains them. The employees are only themselves, and the managers are only the store. If the model is not configured, manual work records and audits can still be performed. Reason: Core business is independent of external model availability.
- Each voice segment is six to sixty seconds long and up to 8 MB. The transcription can be modified before being sent. The recording is only transferred to the requested memory; text/system dictation is used when unavailable. Reason: There are limits to the capabilities of current providers and browsers, and original recordings do not enter account storage.
- WeChat robot rules are concentrated in [Robot Manual](../operations/LANGBOT_WORK_BOT.md): real-time directory, original text evidence, bound identity and restricted intention; multiple people working together, clarifying and learning only. Reason: The plug-in and web confirmation processes are different and cannot be copied into the same preview policy.
- The Web does not register service workers, does not provide installation entrances, and does not persistently cache business responses; it only retains preferences and does not submit drafts for a limited time. Reason: Offline legacy accounts and sensitive caches can mislead write and shared device users.
- Reservations, room/scheduling, customer profiles, software collections, balance write-offs, inventory, refund lines, autopay, payroll taxes/tax returns, and shared device PINs are not part of the existing implementation. Reason: Cleanup does not extend the product scope, and it cannot write the first version of the plan as a delivered feature.
